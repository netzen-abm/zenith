# Research Intelligence Workstream

## Objective
Turn research questions into reproducible, provenance-preserving research workflows.

## Scope
- ResearchQuestion
- ResearchQuery
- ResearchProvider
- ResearchWork
- source identity resolution
- retrieval provenance
- evidence and claim relationships
- reproducibility records
- provider adapters

## Initial provider strategy
Define the canonical contract before adding OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, CORE, DOAJ, Unpaywall or Zotero adapters.

## Acceptance gate
Provider failures, partial results and successful retrievals become durable execution outcomes without creating a parallel lifecycle or security path.
