# Field Acquisition Workstream

## Objective
Create a field-oriented capability for collecting archaeological observations while preserving evidence quality, provenance, consent and offline resilience.

## Scope
- observation and field-session contracts
- media and measurement references
- device metadata with minimization
- offline capture and later synchronization
- location precision policy
- expert review and status transitions
- provenance from observation to evidence

## Safety boundary
Field acquisition must not expose sensitive site coordinates or protected cultural information beyond the caller's authorization and policy scope.

## Acceptance gate
Field mutations use the shared identity, authorization, tenant/RLS, capability, operation, reservation, durable outcome and provenance contracts. Device adapters remain replaceable.
