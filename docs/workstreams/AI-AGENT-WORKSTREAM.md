# AI and Agent Infrastructure Workstream

## Objective
Provide an evidence-grounded AI boundary that can serve independent ZENITH capabilities without becoming an authority over historical truth.

## Rules
AI may retrieve, classify, translate, summarize, compare, suggest, detect, link and generate hypotheses.

AI must preserve source provenance, distinguish evidence from interpretation, expose uncertainty and identify generated or inferred content.

AI must not silently convert inference into historical fact or bypass authorization.

## Architecture
AI requests enter through the shared capability and operation boundary. Model/provider selection remains replaceable.

## Acceptance gate
Every AI action is attributable to an authorized identity and operation and records sufficient provenance for reproducibility.
