# ZENITH — Security, Federation & UX Baseline

## Security architecture
Privacy by Design, Safety by Design and Secure by Default are architecture invariants.

Core controls:
- least privilege
- data minimization
- policy-mediated access
- tenant isolation
- explicit audit
- provenance by default
- retention and revocability
- public-safe projections
- separation of sensitive precise geometry from public/generalized representation

## Sensitive heritage
Sensitive coordinates may require generalization, restricted precision or denial depending on purpose, authorization and policy obligations. Public discovery must never become an accidental disclosure channel for looting-enabling precision.

## Community and cultural knowledge
Human remains, sacred/restricted knowledge, vulnerable-community information and consent-bound interviews are policy-sensitive. Rights and consent must travel with the relevant material and must constrain downstream use.

## AI
AI receives only the minimum authorized context. AI outputs must preserve provenance and epistemic status and remain distinguishable as generated inference. AI cannot bypass the same authorization boundary applied to ordinary users/tools.

## UX / IA principle
Routes and screens are projections of capabilities. The UX must not create an alternate security or domain model. Public, research, sensitive-heritage, AI and federation journeys all terminate at the same shared policy/evidence boundaries.

## Federation
Federated sources retain their own authority. ZENITH records the relationship, external identifier, provenance, rights and synchronization/conflict state rather than silently absorbing external records as native facts.
