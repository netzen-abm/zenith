# Pattanam and Keezhadi — Research Baseline

## Source artifact
This document preserves the research artifact supplied in the pre-implementation architecture package. It is a source-derived baseline, not a replacement for primary excavation publications or current institutional records.

## Pattanam
The source research describes Pattanam in North Paravur, Ernakulam, as an archaeological location associated with ancient Muziris and reports multidisciplinary excavations beginning in 2007 involving KCHR and ASI. It describes evidence spanning the Iron Age, Early Historic period and later occupation, including structural remains, ring-wells, pottery, coins, beads, a teak canoe and a brick wharf with mooring features, alongside Roman-era and West Asian ceramics.

## Keezhadi
The research artifact uses Keezhadi alongside Pattanam to examine how archaeological evidence can revise narratives of South Indian history. The ecosystem therefore treats the vertical as interdisciplinary rather than as a single-site catalogue.

## ZENITH use
Pattanam/Muziris is the first proving vertical for ZENITH because it can exercise:
- archaeology and excavation evidence
- maritime and trade history
- spatial and temporal modelling
- objects/material culture
- evidence/provenance chains
- competing interpretations
- federation with institutional heritage resources
- interdisciplinary research workflows

## Epistemic caution
The research artifact contains historical claims and references. Implementation must model such statements as source-grounded claims with provenance and epistemic status rather than converting narrative prose directly into unqualified facts.
